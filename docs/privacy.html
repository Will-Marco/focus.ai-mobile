Qisqacha: Focus AI ro‘yxatdan o‘tmasdan, internetsiz to‘liq ishlaydi. Mehmon rejimida hech qanday ma’lumot qurilmangizdan chiqmaydi. Ro‘yxatdan o‘tsangiz — faqat sinxronizatsiya va jamoa funksiyalari uchun zarur ma’lumot saqlanadi. Biz reklama ko‘rsatmaymiz, ma’lumotingizni sotmaymiz va uchinchi tomon analitika yoki kuzatuv (tracking) vositalarini ishlatmaymiz.

1. Mehmon rejimi — ma’lumot yig‘ilmaydi

Ilovani ochganingizda u mehmon rejimida ishlaydi. Odatlaringiz, sessiyalaringiz, statistikangiz va sozlamalaringiz faqat qurilmangizda (local baza) saqlanadi. Bu rejimda serverga hech narsa yuborilmaydi.

2. Ro‘yxatdan o‘tsangiz

Ro‘yxatdan o‘tish ixtiyoriy — u faqat qurilmalar orasida sinxronizatsiya va jamoa (Focus Rooms) funksiyalari uchun kerak.

Hisob ma’lumotlari

  • Telefon raqamingiz — hisobni aniqlash uchun
  • Ismingiz — Telegram profilingizdan olinadi (ism yoki foydalanuvchi nomi)
  • Parolingiz shifrlangan holda saqlanadi va bizga ko‘rinmaydi

Ilova ma’lumotlari (sinxronizatsiya)

  • Odatlar — nomi, ikonkasi, rangi, davri va maqsad soni
  • Sessiyalar — davomiyligi, maqsadi, boshlanish/tugash vaqti, yakunlanganligi va “Away” (telefon yuztuban) vaqti
  • Jamoa — a’zo bo‘lgan guruhlaringiz va ulardagi faollik

Odat nomini o‘zingiz yozasiz. Agar unga shaxsiy ma’lumot kiritmaslikni istasangiz, umumiyroq nom tanlashingiz mumkin.

3. AI murabbiy

AI tavsiyasini so‘raganingizda ilova faqat anonim raqamli ko‘rsatkichlarni yuboradi: streak uzunligi, daraja va XP, so‘nggi 30 kundagi umumiy daqiqalar va sessiyalar soni, o‘rtacha sessiya davomiyligi, eng samarali soat, odatlar soni va “Away” daqiqalari.

Ismingiz, telefon raqamingiz va odat nomlaringiz yuborilmaydi — AI faqat raqamlarni ko‘radi. Bu ko‘rsatkichlar tavsiya olish uchun Google Gemini xizmatiga uzatiladi va saqlanmaydi.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 July 2026

In short: Focus AI works fully offline and without an account. In guest mode no data leaves your device. If you create an account, we store only what syncing and the group features require. We show no ads, we do not sell your data, and we use no third-party analytics or tracking.

1. Guest mode — no data collected

The app opens in guest mode. Your habits, sessions, statistics and settings are stored on your device only. Nothing is sent to a server in this mode.

2. If you create an account

Registration is optional and exists only for cross-device sync and the group (Focus Rooms) features.

Account data

  • Phone number — identifies your account
  • Name — taken from your Telegram profile (first name or username)
  • Your password is stored hashed and is never visible to us

App data (sync)

  • Habits — name, icon, colour, period and target count
  • Sessions — duration, target, start and end time, completion state and “Away” time (phone face-down)
  • Groups — the groups you join and your activity in them

Habit names are written by you. If you would rather not put personal details in them, choose a more general name.

3. AI coach

When you request AI advice, the app sends anonymous numeric metrics only: streak length, level and XP, total minutes and session counts over the last 30 days, average session length, most productive hour, number of habits, and “Away” minutes.

Your name, phone number and habit names are never sent — the model sees numbers only. These metrics are passed to Google Gemini to generate the advice and are not retained.
