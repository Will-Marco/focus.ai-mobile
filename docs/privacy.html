Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 July 2026

The short version: Focus AI does not require an account. Use it without one and your data never leaves your device. We run no ads, we do not sell your data, and we embed no third-party tracking.

Using the app without an account

The app opens in guest mode. Your habits, sessions, statistics and settings live on your phone alone — we hold no record of them.

If you create an account

Registration is optional. It exists for two things: syncing your data across devices and joining focus rooms.

What we take from you

  • Phone number — identifies your account
  • Name — taken from your Telegram profile
  • Password — stored hashed; we never see it

What gets synced

  • Your habits — name, icon, colour and target
  • Your sessions — when they started, how long they ran, whether you finished
  • Which focus rooms you belong to

You write the habit names yourself — if you would rather not put personal details in them, pick something more general.

What the AI coach sees

When you ask for advice, only numbers travel: streak length, your level, total minutes over the last 30 days, average session length, your most productive hour and similar figures.

Your name, number and habit titles are never sent. The model does not know who you are — it sees statistics only. Those figures go to Google Gemini and are not retained once the answer comes back.

Deleting your data

To have your account and everything tied to it removed, write to mr.marco0101@gmail.com — we handle it within 30 days. Uninstalling the app clears the data held on your phone.

Children

The app is not built for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.
