Habit-building focus timer

It rewards you for putting your phone down

Most focus apps expect you to keep staring at the screen. Focus AI inverts that — the physical position of the phone becomes the interface.

🛌Place it face-down — “Away”

The sensor picks it up, the screen goes dark, the timer keeps running and you earn double XP for that time.

🔄Turn it sideways — “Focus Clock”

The screen becomes a large desk clock, stays awake, and system Do Not Disturb switches on.

Also included

  • Daily, weekly and monthly habits — the count resets on each period boundary
  • Streaks, a heatmap, XP levels and achievement badges
  • Focus rooms — see who is in a session right now
  • An AI coach that reads your anonymous stats and gives tailored advice
  • Six ambient background tracks
  • Fully offline — no account, no internet required
