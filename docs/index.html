Focus timer

Put the phone down.
That’s where focus starts.

Other apps compete for your screen time. Focus AI does the opposite — it notices when you put the phone down, and rewards you for it.

Two gestures, two modes

No buttons involved. How you set the phone down is the command.

Lay it face-down

The screen goes dark, the timer keeps running. Every minute spent away from the phone counts double.

Turn it sideways

The screen becomes a desk clock and stays awake. The phone falls silent — Do Not Disturb switches on by itself.

After the session

Streaks and stats

Consecutive days, a heatmap, levels and achievement badges.

Any rhythm

Daily, weekly or monthly targets. The count resets itself when the period ends.

Focus rooms

See who is in a session right now. You are not doing this alone.

AI coach

Reads your statistics and tells you when you are actually at your sharpest.

Ambient sound

Six background tracks, from rainfall to a crackling fire.

Works offline

No account needed. Open it and start straight away.

Requirements

Android 7.0 or later · iOS 15.1 or later.
“Away” mode needs a device with a motion sensor.
